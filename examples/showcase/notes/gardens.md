## what a garden is

A digital garden is a website that grows the way notes do: linked, unfinished, and revisited. Its notes are written to be read, but they are never quite done. ^unfinished

## what to plant

- notes that link to one another
- pictures of ideas, written as text ^pictures
- things a reader can poke at
